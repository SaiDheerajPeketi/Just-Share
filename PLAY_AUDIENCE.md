# Just Share Play audience decision

**Candidate age groups:** 13–15, 16–17, and 18+\
**Not selected:** 5 and under, 6–8, and 9–12

Just Share can safely serve teenagers and adults when used to transfer lawful files. It has no ads,
public profiles, social feed, discovery directory, gambling, or public content catalog. Transfers
require nearby-device actions or a deliberately shared Remote connection code.

The under-13 groups are excluded from the first release because users can exchange arbitrary files,
the Remote service uses a persistent anonymous device/quota identifier, and the current candidate
does not provide a neutral age screen, parent-controlled child mode, content moderation, or
child-specific Firebase/RevenueCat behavior. A broader Families release would require a dedicated
child mode and separate review; it must not be enabled solely to increase reach.

## Store and policy requirements

- Describe the app as a file-transfer utility, not a place to discover people or content.
- Do not use child-directed artwork or imply transferred content is curated or moderated.
- Explain Bluetooth, nearby Wi-Fi, camera/QR, media, notification, and foreground-service access at
  the moment each feature needs it.
- Never collect or log file names, paths, content, peer identities, codes, tokens, or IP addresses.
- Reassess the audience whenever discovery, profiles, messaging, cloud storage, or public sharing is
  introduced.
